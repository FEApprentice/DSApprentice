# Exploring the impacts on university acceptance rates in the UK
This project intends to explore how different factors impact university acceptance rates in the UK. The factors include region, year, age and gender. The aim is to review the different attributes listed, and review whether they cause a positive or negative impact on acceptance. This can be used in a wider context to identify specific demographics that may be less likely to attend university.
